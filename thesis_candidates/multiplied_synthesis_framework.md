## 2. How Each Angle Contributes to the Master Thesis

1. **Candidate 1 Contributes the Physics & Invariance Backbone (Layer 1):**
   - Solves the environmental domain shift crisis.
   - Prevents the system from memorizing room-specific static reflections by projecting raw channel data into coordinate-invariant velocity profiles.
2. **Candidate 2 Contributes the Multi-User Filter (Layer 2):**
   - Bridges the gap between single-person laboratory demonstrations and realistic multi-person homes.
   - Decomposes superimposed RF reflections into individual occupant streams before feeding them to downstream task models.
3. **Candidate 3 Contributes the Safety Logic & Digital Health Engine (Layers 3B & 4):**
   - Provides contactless respiration tracking during sedentary rest, solving the false absence alert problem.
   - Governs the system with the 5-state Finite State Machine and enforces that fall impact emergencies are never improperly suppressed by breathing.
4. **Candidate 4 Contributes the Resident Authentication Engine (Layer 3A):**
   - Replaces flawed closed-set classifiers with few-shot open-set gait metric learning.
   - Authenticates family members within seconds of walking while rejecting unknown intruders without needing intruder training data.
5. **Candidate 5 Contributes the Real-World Hardware Feasibility (Layer 0):**
   - Guarantees that the entire multi-task system can actually be deployed on $5 bare-metal microcontrollers with 512 KB SRAM.
   - Proves $<45$ ms real-time inference latency under INT8 quantization without expensive cloud computing or privacy leakage.

## 3. Why This Multiplied Strategy Is Winning for an Undergraduate Thesis

This modular formulation provides unprecedented strategic flexibility when presenting your research to your supervisor:

### Strategy A: The Unified Master Thesis (Comprehensive)
You present the complete multi-layer framework as your thesis. This demonstrates extraordinary architectural maturity and system-level thinking rarely seen in undergraduate work.

### Strategy B: The Deep-Dive Modular Thesis (Targeted)
If your supervisor feels the full system is too broad for an undergraduate project, you can simply zoom into **any single layer** (Candidate 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5) and make that your complete thesis! Because each candidate is already fully fleshed out with its own literature review, mathematics, datasets, and benchmarks in this directory, you can pivot in seconds to whatever track your supervisor prefers.
