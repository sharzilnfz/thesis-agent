WiFi Sensing Thesis Dashboard

Start Here — the thesis question in 3 sentences

1. Everyday WiFi signals bounce off people, so they can sense movement and breathing without wearables (wearables means devices you must wear on your body). 2. The hard part is that accuracy drops in a new room, with a new person, or on new hardware, because the signal patterns change. 3. This thesis asks which training and testing methods make WiFi sensing honestly reliable across unseen people, rooms, and devices.

Glossary (each in 10 words or fewer)

CSI
Channel State Information: WiFi signal measurements per antenna.
LOSO
Leave-One-Subject-Out: test on a person never trained on.
LOEO
Leave-One-Environment-Out: test in a room never trained on.
BVP
Body-coordinate Velocity Profile: movement map independent of location.
DFS
Doppler Frequency Shift: frequency change caused by moving bodies.
open-set
Handling strangers: system admits people it never saw before.
